## J4 vs. my H3 — one instrument or two

**Two separate instruments — they don't share a trigger axis.** J4's mechanism is a *read-position* defect: `ReadAt`'s modulo wraps `timeR` back into the buffer once `dtim`+`dwid` push it past capacity, aliasing to a short, unrelated lag. That's driven by **`dtim`** (how close `baseSeconds` alone already sits to `capacity`) crossed with `dwid`, and it corrupts `dL`/`dR` directly at the read, upstream of everything else — so it would show up at the existing tap point (`Process`'s `DelayWetPair`), or even more directly at the raw `ReadAt` output, and sweeping `dtim` alongside the pinned `dwid` grid is the right instrument for it.

My H3 concern is a *limiter-dynamics* defect: `wetLimiterL`/`wetLimiterR` engaging asymmetrically now that cross-feed no longer holds L/R together. That's driven by **signal level and Feedback/Freeze drive** — whatever actually pushes a channel's per-sample value near the limiter's threshold — which is a different axis from `dtim`. The pinned instrument's own amplitude (`0.5·uniform`) and Feedback operating point (0.7) may never approach the limiter's engagement region at all, in which case sweeping `dtim`×`dwid` at that same amplitude would sail past the limiter concern without ever exercising it, regardless of how high `dwid` and `dtim` go. It also needs a different *statistic*: an aggregate correlation/RMS-ratio over a 12000-sample stationary window can average away a transient per-channel limiter divergence that's real and audible frame-to-frame; catching it needs either the limiters' own gain-reduction state per channel or a short-window (not whole-burst) level-difference metric.

So: one shared sweep axis (`dwid`, for "is the pair actually decorrelated") plus two independent trigger axes and two independent taps — `dtim` × the existing wet-pair tap for J4, and level/Feedback drive × a limiter-internal or short-window tap for H3. A single measurement at high `dtim` and high `dwid` covers J4; it does not by itself cover H3 unless the level is also pushed into the limiters' engagement region.
